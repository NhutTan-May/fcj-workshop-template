During my internship at **Amazon Web Services Viet Nam Company Limited** (**Workforce Bootcamp - First Cloud AI Journey**) from **14/09/2026** to **14/12/2026**, I had the opportunity to experience a real-world engineering environment and learn practical skills beyond university coursework.  
Over this period, I completed the 12-week AWS hands-on learning track covering core infrastructure (VPC, EC2, IAM, S3, RDS), automation (CloudFormation, Lambda, IAM Identity Center), containers (Docker, ECS, EKS), Data Lake analytics (Athena, QuickSight), and AI/ML services (Rekognition, Bedrock, SageMaker), while also attending community events and writing technical summary blogs on AWS features. Through these activities, I improved my skills in cloud deployment, reading English technical documentation, teamwork, and technical reporting.

### Needs Improvement

* Go deeper into AWS networking, security, and IAM permission mechanics rather than stopping at guided lab steps, since my primary academic background is in AI and cloud infrastructure took time to get comfortable with.
* Practice more systematic troubleshooting and debugging habits when configurations fail (checking logs thoroughly and isolating root causes faster before asking for help).
* Improve concise, structured technical communication when explaining problems or collaborating in group discussions.
* Manage time more evenly between university coursework and weekly cloud lab targets to avoid cramming tasks near the weekend.
